FLUXFORGE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

FLUXFORGE is a single-player arcade game. It does not require an account and does not include ads or analytics SDKs in the code shipped with this project.

Data we store on your device

The game stores a local high score in your browser or app storage (localStorage). That value never leaves the device unless you back up the app yourself.

Data we do not collect

  • No names, emails, or accounts
  • No location
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash or usage telemetry in the default build

Permissions

Vibration may be used for impact feedback. Motion sensors may be used as an optional FLUX gesture. Both features work entirely on-device.
